# VoiceOver inventory

Scope: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-voiceover-design.md`. Capture with `tools/accessibility/capture.sh <udid> <screen-id>`.

## How to check a screen

- **Build** debug (`--configuration=debug_sim_arm64`) and install by whole-`.app` copy (root `CLAUDE.md`, "Updating the
  running simulator after a rebuild").
- **Launch with `--force-voiceover-layout`**: `xcrun simctl launch <udid> ph.telegra.Telegraph --force-voiceover-layout`.
  The simulator never runs VoiceOver, and some screens lay out differently under it (contacts sorted by name, picker
  taps select, story viewer controls). The flag makes `isVoiceOverRunningForLayout` (`Display/Source/Accessibility.swift`)
  return true. It exists in DEBUG builds only.
- **Simulators.**
  - Logged in: iPhone 17 Pro `EA95FB21-AE07-4325-B995-943D36E889C6`.
  - Logged out (welcome, phone, code, sign-up): a separate iPhone 17 Pro Max `E5E21C20-6660-4019-BC32-D7BE43F2C492`,
    launched with `--ui-test` as well (test servers). Never log the main sim out.
- **Capture**: `tools/accessibility/capture.sh <udid> <screen-id>` writes `build/accessibility/<screen-id>/`:
  - `screen.png`;
  - `tree.json`, the AXe `describe-ui` dump. AXe is at `~/.local/opt/axe/axe`; override with `AXE=`;
  - `report.txt`, from `tools/accessibility/axaudit.py`: one line per element (type, label, value, traits, frame), then
    `FLAG` lines.
- **Reading the result.** Flags are hints; the screenshot shows what is missing from the tree. AXe `tap` does not deliver
  touches on these sims: navigate with deep links (`xcrun simctl openurl <udid> 'tg://settings/…'`) or simulator taps,
  one tap at a time with a fresh screenshot before each. To compare two captures, `diff` their `report.txt` element
  lines.
- **Tests**: `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/accessibility/tests` checks the audit script.
  `//submodules/Display:DisplayAccessibilityTests` and `//submodules/ComponentFlow:ComponentFlowTests` check the
  collector and the component convention.

## Open gaps (summary)

Named in the rows below. In short:
- **Charts**: StatsGraphItem charts (channel statistics, Stars stats, revenue) are not readable.
- **Chat-owned surfaces**: the attach-menu gallery and the sticker/emoji category strip (EntityKeyboardTopPanelComponent) are shared with the chat, and the Saved-pane message rows are chat bubbles. They are left to the chat's own pass, as is removing `ChatControllerNode`'s opt-out.
- **Drawn content**: entities and media areas on stories and on the story editor canvas are not elements; Instant View headings and inline links.
- **Gesture-only**: drag-to-reorder handles (chat list, folders); holding a collectible to put it on the QR card.
- **Small**:
  - the passcode lock's entered-digit count;
  - the wallet More menu's Currency value;
  - the Affiliate Programs title and its rows' long-press menu;
  - the News loading skeleton;
  - the camera's scaled-out glass buttons;
  - the selected-users list header order;
  - the edit-profile Description frame.
- **Not reached on this account** (fixed in code where noted): Business setup, wallet connect and key screens, a large group's Members pane, a topic's info, other people's story replies, two-factor, sign-up and Terms (test servers reject codes).
