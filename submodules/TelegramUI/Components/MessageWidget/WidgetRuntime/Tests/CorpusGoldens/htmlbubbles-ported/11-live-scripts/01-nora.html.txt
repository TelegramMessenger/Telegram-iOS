== dom
<html><head><style></style>
</head><body>These cards are <b>scripted messages</b>. Each one carries a <code>&lt;script&gt;</code> that runs in its own JavaScriptCore sandbox. It defines <code>render()</code> (state → HTML) and <code>onAction(name, value)</code>, which is called when you click anything with a <code>data-action</code>. Every click updates the same bubble in place: <code>tween()</code> and <code>animate()</code> move numbers, sizes and colours at up to 60 fps, and <code>transition('pop')</code> or <code>'slide-left'</code> lets the bubble animate natively when its content swaps. Right-click a card and pick <i>Script Log…</i> to see its console.
</body></html>
