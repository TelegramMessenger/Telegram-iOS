Each frame changes ordinary properties: sizes, margins, absolute positions, rgba colours (<code>mix()</code>, <code>alpha()</code>), gradient angles, or a freshly generated SVG image. When a card swaps whole content (a tab, a step, a result), <code>transition('slide-left')</code> or <code>'pop'</code> lets the bubble animate it natively. Animation pauses whenever a card is off screen, and every loop stops once nothing moves.
</body></html>
